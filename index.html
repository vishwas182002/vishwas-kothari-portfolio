About

I build and evaluate machine-learning systems end to end: data, model, testing, and the interface on top. Before CU Boulder I was a researcher at ISRO's Space Applications Centre, working on ocean-color retrieval. I fit Levenberg-Marquardt models to satellite reflectance to estimate chlorophyll and related water properties, validated against 105 samples (R² = 0.987 on reflectance, 0.879 in log-space for chlorophyll-a).

At CU Boulder my coursework and projects center on model evaluation, interpretability, optimization theory, and vision-language models. I build the full stack of a project: dataset, model, evaluation, and dashboard. My focus is on measuring where models fail, not only where they perform.

FinAgent-Eval

02ML Theory · Optimization

Implicit Bias of Adam vs. SGD

0.99cosine similarity between empirical and exact max-margin solutions.

An empirical test of recent theory on optimizer implicit bias: gradient descent converges to the ℓ₂ max-margin solution, Adam to ℓ∞. I verified this across five seeds against exact convex-program solutions, then measured the cost on a spurious-correlation benchmark. SGD dropped 30.2% out of distribution with 3× shortcut reliance, while Adam dropped 13.6%.

5 seeds · CVXPY reference solutions · arXiv:2406.10650
