About

My background runs through two very different rooms. At ISRO's Space Applications Centre I built optimization pipelines to pull clean signal out of noisy satellite sensor data. At CU Boulder the question shifted from what a model does to why it works — and what happens when it quietly stops working.

That's the thread through everything here: I care less about the demo and more about the failure mode. A model nobody can audit is a model nobody should trust, so I build the measurement, the fairness check, and the failure taxonomy alongside the model, not after it.

Away from the screen: cricket, badminton, and a reading habit that leans toward technology, business, and the occasional uncomfortable idea.
