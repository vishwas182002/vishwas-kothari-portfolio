About

I build machine-learning systems that are measured as carefully as they are modeled. My work spans data pipelines, model development, evaluation design, explainability, and the interfaces that make results usable for technical teams.

Before joining CU Boulder for my MS in Computer Science, I worked at ISRO's Space Applications Centre on ocean-color retrieval from satellite reflectance. I developed retrieval models using the Levenberg-Marquardt optimization method to estimate chlorophyll and related water properties, then validated the outputs against 105 measured samples with strong agreement across reflectance and chlorophyll-a.

My current focus is practical and evaluation-heavy: vision-language models, document AI, trustworthy ML, optimizer behavior, and scientific data systems. The throughline is reliability: not only whether a model works, but where it breaks, why it breaks, and what evidence should make us trust it.

01Evaluation · Multimodal · 2026

FinAgent-Eval

4.3×ANLS over the strongest document-AI baseline, 0.6368 vs. 0.1472.

A full evaluation system for vision-language models on financial document question answering. I curated 397 QA pairs from ten sources, compared five models on a single free GPU, and paired aggregate scores with failure analysis. 02ML Theory · Optimization

Implicit Bias of Adam vs. SGD

0.99cosine similarity between empirical and exact max-margin solutions.

A research-grade empirical study of optimizer implicit bias. I tested the theory that gradient descent converges toward the ℓ₂ max-margin solution while Adam moves toward ℓ∞, then verified the behavior across five seeds against exact convex-program solutions. I also measured the downstream cost on a spurious-correlation benchmark, where SGD dropped 30.2% out of distribution with 3× shortcut reliance, while Adam dropped 13.6%.

5 seeds · CVXPY reference solutions · arXiv:2406.10650

04Benchmark · Transfer Learning

Document VQA & Transfer

+22% / -39%in-domain fine-tuning versus generic fine-tuning on the same model and task.

A document-VQA benchmark focused on dense, real-world financial documents rather than simplified benchmark pages.
