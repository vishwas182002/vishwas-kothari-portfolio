FinAgent-Eval

4.3×ANLS over the strongest document-AI baseline (0.6368 vs. 0.1472).

An evaluation harness for vision-language models on document question answering, run end to end on a single free GPU. Five models compared across 397 hand-curated QA pairs from ten sources. ML Theory · Optimization

Implicit Bias of Adam vs. SGD

0.99cosine similarity between empirical and exact max-margin solutions.

An empirical test of recent theory on optimizer implicit bias: gradient descent converges to the ℓ₂ max-margin solution, Adam to ℓ∞. Verified across five seeds against exact convex-program solutions, then measured the cost on a spurious-correlation benchmark — SGD dropped 30.2% out of distribution with 3× shortcut reliance; Adam dropped 13.6%.

5 seeds · CVXPY reference solutions · arXiv:2406.10650

About

My background is in applied ML research and end-to-end system building. At ISRO's Space Applications Centre I worked on ocean-color retrieval, fitting Levenberg–Marquardt models to satellite reflectance to estimate chlorophyll and related water properties, validated against 105 samples (R² = 0.987 on reflectance, 0.879 in log-space for chlorophyll-a).

At CU Boulder my coursework and projects center on model evaluation, interpretability, optimization theory, and vision-language models. I build the full stack of a project — dataset, model, evaluation, and dashboard — and focus on measuring where models fail, not only where they perform.
